EXPLORING AND PREDICTING E-COMMERCE CUSTOMER CHURN AND BASELINE CLASSIFIERS ​

​
E-commerce companies face significant revenue loss due to customer churn (customers leaving or becoming inactive).​
Retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.​
​
Objective of the Project: Predict which customers are likely to churn using historical data.​
​
Revenue & Profitability Impact:​
E-commerce churn exceeds 60% annually ​
Repeat customers represent only 21% of customer base but generate 44% of total revenue​
5% increase in retention → 25-95% increase in profit ​
Losing high-value customers directly impacts Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) ​
​
Acquisition vs. Retention Economics:​
Acquiring new customers is 5-25× more expensive than retaining existing ones ​
Repeat customers spend 67% more than new customers ​
Probability of selling to existing customer: 60-70% – New prospects: only 5-20% success rate​​
Existing customers are 50% more likely to try new products ​
Loyal customers spend 31% more on average ​
